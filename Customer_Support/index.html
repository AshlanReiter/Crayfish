Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our crayfish, products and ordering process.

What type of crayfish do you offer?

We specialize in Australian Red Claw (ARC) crayfish, scientifically known as Cherax quadricarinatus.

What crayfish purchasing options are available?

You can choose from Solo, Duo, Trio, or Kilo/Food Consumption options on our Order Page.

Visit Order Page
What other products do you offer?

We offer feeds and equipment for crayfish farming, including Beginner Setup, Trapalpond with Aerator, Trapalpond Only, and Aerator Only.

How can I place an order?

Visit our Order Page, choose the crayfish or product you want, enter the required order information, and proceed with the payment instructions provided.

Visit Order Page
What information do I need to provide?

The ordering process asks for the required amount or size, recipient's name, phone number, and delivery address.

How do I submit my payment?

Follow the online wallet or bank QR payment instructions shown during the ordering process. After completing the payment, attach a screenshot of your payment before placing your order.

Where can I learn more about crayfish?

Visit our Crayfish 101 page to learn about crayfish history, species, proper care, handling, and breeding.

Visit Crayfish 101
